Working with Me

I embody the writing culture at Amazon and would like to introduce myself through this guide. Before you jump in — I'm a goofball. We spend too much time at work for us not to enjoy it. I love cracking jokes, sharing memes & talking about personal interests.

Ways of Working

Love Challenges from Others

I appreciate people challenging me on my output. I find it's most helpful when our team is each other's biggest critics by challenging the status quo, asking "Why?" 5 times, so the leadership doesn't have to. Once we make a decision, we move forward together.

Listening Before Acting

I tend to listen before I share my opinions to ensure I understand the others' PoV. In doing so, I hope to remove frictions with debates & incorporate as much feedback into my work as possible.

No Surprises

I hope to be predictable. I'm a firm believer in being consistent in principles. I hope my team & stakeholders would be less confused & can easily predict expectations.

Directed Independence

My ideal working style is we define project deliverables & timelines and let each team run with it. I love to get involved and collaborate to remove roadblocks, problem solve, and whiteboard new ideas.

Communication Preferences

Feedback/Growth Ideas — Timely > Periodic

I am open to feedback for my growth. I love learning more about areas where I could have done better with tangible examples. I would love to get it in a timely manner when something happens so that I can incorporate it into my daily work quickly for improvement.

Direct > Indirect Communication

I appreciate direct communication rather than indirect communication. I tend to misunderstand indirect communication. But I come from a culture known for high-context and indirect communication style. This might come out unconsciously in my own style. I want to be respectful and polite especially for those I don't know well or who are more senior to me.

More Communication > Less

I like to send & receive updates on my projects for feedback. Soft responses like "will read later" or "sounds good" let me know that emails & projects have value. If they don't have value, then feedback is welcome. Email vs. Slack: I strongly value well-structured writing for any long documents or use cases.

Getting Things Done

Done > Deep Dive

For certain questions, I prefer taking action rather than over-processing the logic of concepts. I do value thorough analysis, but I balance it with a bias for action.

Data Speaks Volumes

I am very data-driven but also skeptical of data. I value structured plans and it is important to understand the end-to-end funnel and how the pieces connect.

Visual Methodology

I am a visual person. The best way to bring clarity is using visuals instead of only text — it allows me to see how concepts connect. Complex requirements need visual representations that are immediately clear.

Structured Plans

I value structure and clear planning. It is important to understand the end-to-end picture so we can identify dependencies and work efficiently as a team.
